Beacon

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Beacon lets you tell the friends you choose that you're up for something, now or later in the week. This page explains what Beacon keeps, what it looks at but doesn't keep, who helps run it, and how to delete everything. Beacon is run by Warren Porter, an individual in the UK. Contact: warren24237@gmail.com.

What Beacon keeps

  • Your first name and mobile number. Your number is only visible to people you're friends with on Beacon, so they can message or call you.
  • Your friends, friend requests and circles. Circles are private: only you see them.
  • Your beacons: what you're up for, when, an optional note, and optional places (a Google place reference, or text you typed). Only the friends you send a beacon to can see it.
  • "I'm in"s and place preferences. Only the person who lit the beacon sees who's in and where they'd prefer.
  • Find a date requests and answers (which dates you're free), visible to the people in that request.
  • A notification address for your phone, so Beacon can send you notifications.

Beacon has no passwords or email sign-in. Joining creates an anonymous account tied to the app on your phone.

What Beacon looks at but doesn't keep

  • Contacts, only if you allow it. Beacon reads your contacts' phone numbers and sends them to Beacon's server, which checks them against Beacon's members to show who you can add. The numbers are compared and then discarded, never stored. Names from your contacts never leave your phone.
  • Calendar, only if you allow it, for Find a date. Beacon checks your calendar on your phone to tick the dates you're free. Your events never leave your phone; only your ticks are shared.

Who helps run Beacon

  • Supabase stores Beacon's data, in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Expo delivers app updates and sends notifications to your phone, via Google Firebase on Android.
  • Google Places powers place search. When you search for a place, the text you type is sent to Google to find matches. Beacon keeps only Google's reference for the place you pick.

Beacon has no ads, no tracking or analytics, and never sells or shares your data with anyone else.

How long it's kept

Your data is kept while you have a Beacon account. Beacons go out at their end time and are no longer shown. Deleting your account removes everything listed above for good.

Deleting your account

In the app: Friends, then Delete my account at the bottom. Or see how to delete your account without the app.

Your rights

You can ask what data Beacon holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or complain. Email warren24237@gmail.com. You can also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Beacon is not meant for anyone under 13.
