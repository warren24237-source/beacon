Delete your Beacon account

In the app (quickest)

Open Beacon, tap Friends, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account, then Delete. It happens straight away.

Without the app

If you've already uninstalled Beacon, email warren24237@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Beacon account", your first name and the mobile number you joined with. Your account will be deleted within 7 days and you'll get a reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

Everything: your name, mobile number, friends, requests, circles, beacons, places, "I'm in"s, place preferences, Find a date requests and answers, and your notification address. Nothing is kept. Nobody is told.
